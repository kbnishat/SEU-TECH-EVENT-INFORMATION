SEU Tech Event Information

About the Event

The SEU Tech Event is an annual technology conference organized by Saudi Electronic University. The event brings together students, faculty, and industry professionals to share knowledge and explore the latest trends in technology and computing.

This year the event will focus on topics like artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and software development. It is open to all SEU students and staff.

Venue: SEU Main Campus | Date: August 22-24, 2026

Event Goals

The main goals of this event are to encourage students to engage with real-world technology, to connect with professionals in the field, and to showcase student projects and research.

Through workshops, talks, and exhibitions, students will gain practical skills and build connections that help them grow in their academic and professional careers.

Who Can Participate

  • All SEU undergraduate students
  • Graduate students
  • SEU faculty members
  • Invited guests from industry

How to Prepare
